# Jadawl Team owner guide

Team owners manage people and workspace access from **Organizations**. The
owner is a single organization role and consumes one purchased seat.

Invite a person with their verified email, choose Member or Administrator, and
share the generated invitation token through the approved channel. The invited
person must sign in with that same email before accepting. Pending invitations
do not consume seats; acceptance rechecks capacity.

Owners can add an existing Jadawl user, suspend/reactivate members, remove
members, assign members to managed workspaces, and transfer ownership to an
active member. Administrators can manage members within their authority but
cannot appoint or remove administrators. The owner cannot be removed or
suspended.

Suspending a member removes their managed workspace access while retaining
their seat. Removing them releases the seat and preserves their records. A
workspace with unrelated existing users requires a general administrator's
preview before it can be bound to the organization.

Billing is separate from people management. Team seat purchases and renewal
state are handled in **Billing**; a complimentary grant is clearly labelled
and does not represent a payment. During a renewal grace period, existing
members can continue permitted reads. Once access becomes restricted, recover
the paid subscription or ask a general administrator to assign a valid grant.
